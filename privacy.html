Privacy policy

Last updated 13 October 2026

The short version: the app sends nothing anywhere, the website only counts visits with Google Analytics if you accept the cookie banner, and the only other third party involved is the store that takes your payment.

The app

Many Displays runs entirely on your Mac. It makes no network connections of its own, has no analytics, no crash reporting and no account. There are three exceptions, all of which you trigger:

  • Updates. When you choose "Check for Updates…", or if you turn on automatic update checks, the app downloads a small file from manydisplays.com that lists the available versions, and the update itself from GitHub. Your Mac's IP address is visible to those servers as part of any web request. The update framework (Sparkle) sends the app version and your macOS version so it can offer a compatible build. No system profile or identifier is sent.
  • Licence activation. Licence keys are verified on your Mac with a public key; entering a key sends nothing anywhere.
  • Links. Items in the menu that open this website, GitHub or an email open them in your browser or mail app.

Everything the app stores stays in your home folder:

  • ~/Library/Application Support/ManyDisplays/layouts.json holds your window layouts, including window titles, which are used to re-link windows after an app restarts.
  • ~/Library/Logs/ManyDisplays.log is a plain-text log of what the app did, with display names and window titles. You can delete it whenever you like, and nobody sees it unless you send it to us for support.

The app needs the Accessibility permission to move windows. It uses it only for that, and it does not read window contents, keystrokes or clipboard.

This website

manydisplays.com is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses Google Analytics 4 to count visits and see which pages are read. Analytics cookies are only set if you accept the banner shown on your first visit; if you decline, Google receives only cookieless, aggregated pings and no identifier is stored on your device. IP addresses are anonymised, there is no advertising, and the data is not sold or shared beyond Google's processing of it (see Google's privacy policy). You can change your choice by clearing this site's data in your browser. No other trackers or advertising scripts run on the site. GitHub, as the hosting provider, may log requests (IP address, user agent, time) as described in GitHub's privacy statement. Downloads are served from GitHub Releases under the same terms.

Buying a licence

Purchases are handled by Stripe (Stripe Managed Payments), which acts as the merchant of record. Stripe collects and processes your name, email address, billing country and payment details, issues the invoice and handles VAT and sales tax. We never see your card number. We receive your email address and the purchase date so we can issue your licence key, email it to you, answer support requests and process refunds; the key itself is derived from those two facts and nothing is stored beyond what Stripe keeps. Stripe's handling of your data is described in their privacy policy.

Support email

If you write to support@manydisplays.com, we keep the conversation for as long as needed to help you and to improve the app. If you attach a log or diagnostics output, please check it first; it can contain window titles. We do not share it with anyone.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time what we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it, by emailing the address above. For data held by Stripe, contact them directly or ask us and we will forward the request.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with a new date. The site is in a public Git repository, so every change is visible.
